    Method, factors and sources

    Everything runs in your browser. Each result is activity × emission factor, divided by household size where the activity is shared (electricity, LPG). You can edit the grid factor and tariff under Home energy → Advanced.

    CO₂ versus CO₂e. The total is labelled CO₂e for convenience, but it mixes two kinds of factor. Grid electricity, road fuel, buses, metro and LPG factors count carbon dioxide only, so those parts may slightly understate full CO₂e. Diet, shopping, waste, flights, rail and digital factors are rounded estimates that already include other greenhouse gases. The “Measures” column shows which is which.

    Vehicle factors are approximate representative values, not exact for every vehicle. Real emissions depend on vehicle type, engine size, age and driving conditions.

    Sources

    • CEA, CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector: grid emission factor. Default 0.675 kg CO₂/kWh is the FY 2025-26 weighted average (Version 22.0); the earlier 0.71 was FY 2024-25.
    • India GHG Program, Road Transport Emission Factors: two-wheelers, cars, three-wheelers, city bus.
    • TERI, per-passenger emissions by mode in Delhi: car fuel factors and occupancy.
    • ITF/OECD, Life-Cycle Assessment of Passenger Transport: An Indian Case Study: electric versus fossil vehicles, metro.
    • UNEP, Enabling Sustainable Lifestyles in a Climate Emergency (2021), citing Akenji et al. (2021), 1.5-Degree Lifestyles, Hot or Cool Institute: the three comparison lines, all consumption-based lifestyle footprints in t CO₂e per person (India average 3.0 t and world average 4.6 t, 2019 data; 1.5 °C target 2.5 t for 2030). They are not comparable with territorial CO₂ figures such as national per-capita CO₂ statistics.
    • LPG uses the IPCC default of about 3.0 kg CO₂ per kg. Diet, shopping, flights, rail, streaming and tree-absorption values are rounded estimates from published ranges.

    Limits

    • One national grid factor is used. State-level factors differ, so edit it if you know yours.
    • Rupee figures are rough national averages for petrol, LPG, fares and tariff. They show direction, not a quote.
    • Embodied emissions of vehicles and appliances are not counted, except for phones and laptops.
    • Results are indicative estimates. Their accuracy has not been validated against measured data.
    • The “demo history” button creates sample bars from your current numbers so you can see the tracker. It is not real history.
